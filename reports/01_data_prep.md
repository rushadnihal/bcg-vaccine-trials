## Data

Source: `metadat::dat.bcg` (Colditz et al., 1994, *JAMA*). 13 trials of the BCG vaccine against tuberculosis, with 357,347 participants in total.

## Effect measure

Log risk ratio (vaccinated vs control), computed with `metafor::escalc(measure = "RR")`. RR < 1 means a lower TB risk in the vaccinated group.

## Summary

- Trials with RR < 1: **11 of 13**
- Trial-level RR range: 0.20 to 1.56
- Absolute latitude range: 13 to 55 degrees
- Allocation methods: alternate = 2, random = 7, systematic = 4
